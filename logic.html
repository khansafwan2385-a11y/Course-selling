This comprehensive workshop will take you through the fundamentals and advanced concepts of logic building, focusing on problem-solving and algorithmic thinking.

  • Introduction to problem-solving and logic building
  • Understanding algorithms and flowcharts
  • Basic logic structures: sequence, selection, and iteration
  • Problem-solving strategies

Day 2: Intermediate Logic Building

  • Advanced problem-solving techniques
  • Implementation of logic in programming languages like C, C++, or Python
  • Practice exercises and real-world problem-solving
  • Complex algorithms and flowcharting
